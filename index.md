# BikeTO Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-06

BikeTO plans bike trips in Toronto and guides you turn by turn.
It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
This policy explains what the app uses, what leaves your phone, and why.

## Your location

BikeTO uses your location, only while you use it, to show where you are, plan trips from there and guide you.
During turn-by-turn guidance it keeps using your location with the screen locked, until the trip ends.
BikeTO never asks for your location at all times.

To plan a trip, the app sends its start and end points to BikeTO's trip planner, which runs on Google Cloud in Toronto.
The planner works out the routes and sends them back.
It does not store your trips, and its logs leave out positions.

## The map

To show bike lanes, Bike Share stations, bike parking and repair shops, the app asks BikeTO's server for the ones in the area of the map you are looking at.
Place search ("Where to?") uses Apple's MapKit, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
The map itself comes from Apple Maps.

## Server logs

Like most online services, Google Cloud keeps a record of each request to BikeTO's server, with its time and your device's IP address, for up to 30 days.
These records are used only to keep the service running and secure.

## Repair shops

When you view a repair shop, ask for directions to it or call it, BikeTO adds one to that shop's count for the day.
The counts say nothing about who you are.

## On your phone

Your choices (whether you ride your own bike or Bike Share, which streets you prefer, and your settings) are stored only on your phone.
Test versions of BikeTO may keep a log of your rides on your phone, for improving guidance; it leaves your phone only if you choose to share it.

## Data from others

Bike lanes, parking and other city data come from the City of Toronto's Open Data and OpenStreetMap; Bike Share station data comes from Bike Share Toronto; transit data comes from the TTC.
BikeTO sends none of your information to them.

## Children

BikeTO is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
